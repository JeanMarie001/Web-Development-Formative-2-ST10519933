# Part 2 Details

I used css to style my Website for OBLIVIAN.

I made the index page simplistic and straight to the point. For the navigation panel, i added a box with i tiny white borner which has all the web pages in it. I have the web pages laid out Horizontally on the right side of the screen.

The fonts i chose to use for my website are Georgia and serif as they stick with the dark retro theme I was going for. The colours Orange, brown, cream, and black also go with the theme i wanted for the website. It gives off that vintage aesthetic which is mostly the kind of clothes Oblivian will be releasing.

I put the logo in Bold on the left side on the navigation panel.
In the index page,  right below the navigation, i added a box with a border which cotains the logo and a quick easy access to the shopping section making the users experience much easier to navigate through the website. In that box there contains the slogan for OBVLIVIAN which will immediately convey what we are about to the user and is what will fully  capture their attention. Which is why i made the caption orange.

Below  that i added the new drop  latest collection from Oblivian so the users could see the most recent and brand new drop. This saves the user from scrolling looking for the new items mixed with the older ones.

And below that are the  images of the new releases within boxes and borders with their names and prices right below  it.

And at the very bottom of the page i added What oblivian is about. We are a very realistic brand. We know we wont appeal to everyone which is why when writing what we were about i was specifically addressing the target audiance, being individuas who are not afraid to stand out and like to express themselves in ways that show who they are exactly.

I also added a bottom navigation making the users experience easier when navigating through the website. They wont need to scroll  all the way back up just to go to tthe next page, it will easily be accessible from the bottom of the screen aswell as the top

The About Page Seemlessly separates the different topics talked about from Oblivian with the Bold orange headers. From who oblivian is, to our story, to the philosophy behind the brand, to what we create. The what we create section was more of a list format, as i divided them within their individual borders. 

The shop page is centered around the products and are neatly organised within their boxes and borders. The borders on this page as well as the other pages have slightly rounded corners.

The bottom of this shop page also gives the users hands on experiences and helps them connect closer with the brand by adding videos and photos of behind the scenes, photoshoots, and more. Here there is a youtube video of a photoshoot shot for the brand.

The enquiry page is oragnised in a way that is easy for the user to understand any issue they may be facing with the products or the website even and has an enquiry for properly structured with boxes making it easy for them to get in contact with the company.

The contact page is set up in a way that provides you with all the information needed to get in contact with oblivian about whatever it is that you need and I even added a border with space to include your name, email, the subject and the message you want to send. This provides a more personal experience for the user as you are now directly requesting for something that you need done for yourself secifically.
